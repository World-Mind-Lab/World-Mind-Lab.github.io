Research Overview

Our lab focuses on Physical World Modeling, with the goal of enabling artificial intelligence (AI) systems to understand and interact with the physical world. We pursue this goal by modeling interaction, future dynamics, geometric structure, and visual appearance. Our research integrates embodied AI, generative modeling, and 3D vision, with applications in robotics, world simulation, and 3D reconstruction.

The specific research directions involve the following major areas:

  • World Models: including world action models and multimodal world models across vision, language, and action.
  • 3D Reconstruction & Generation: including physically grounded 3D generation for robotics.
  • Vision-Language Planning: including explainable vision-language-action (VLA) systems.
  • Real-World Data and Deployment: including universal data capture and deployment on robot-arm, humanoid, etc.
